Explanatory notes

The previous edition of DIN 522, issued in September 1975, was already then in substantial agreement with the international standard in preparation. The international standard was published in May 1977, it being ISO 4759/III "Tolerances for fasteners; Part III: Washers for metric bolts, screws and nuts with thread diameters from 1 up to and including 150 mm, product grades A and C".

This ISO Standard caused Standard DIN 522 to be again revised, with the result that the contents of the ISO Standard were incorporated almost completely. As is evident from the introduction to the above revised edition, the hitherto existing finishes m (medium) and g (coarse) were replaced by ISO product grades A and C without causing material changes to the associated features (tolerances, etc.).

Product grade F was accepted as a supplement to both ISO product grades. Washers of this product grade are required, for example, in precision engineering where automated assembly takes place and with regard to feeding devices, tolerances as close as possible are necessary.

Standard ISO 4759/III does not contain any data on surface roughness and in particular, any specifications for acceptance testing. For this reason, the specifications from the September 1975 edition of DIN 522 were adopted with virtually no changes. Values for AQL have been added solely for product grade F.

The values of the peak-to-valley height (table 3) were converted from R1 to Rz in line with DIN 4768, without causing any changes.

Since a technical need could not be determined, for product grade C values for the permissible unflatness (sub-clause 5.2.3) as specified in ISO 4759/III were not incorporated into DIN 522.

For washers made from stainless steel, product grade A allows a maximum permissible unflatness 2 c, due to increased springback of this material which cannot entirely be levelled by straightening.
